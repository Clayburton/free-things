Frequently asked

Are these really free?
Yes — genuinely free. The Kontakt instruments download straight away, no email needed. DEM-Osc goes through a free checkout, so you’ll enter an email and we’ll send it over.
What do I need to run them?
DEM-Osc runs on macOS as VST3/AU. The Kontakt instruments run in Kontakt — some require the full version of Kontakt 6+.
Who made them?
Clay and Kelsy — the same duo behind the paid Osc Collection and our original music.
